# Contributing

Fjernsyn is a personal project. I am not looking for contributions, and this repository takes no issues.

If you want to help, the projects Fjernsyn builds on are a good place to start:

- [FreeTube](https://github.com/FreeTubeApp/FreeTube), where most of this app comes from.
- [Invidious](https://github.com/iv-org/invidious), an alternative way to access YouTube.
- [YouTube.js](https://github.com/LuanRT/YouTube.js), the library behind direct access to YouTube.
- [yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp), which handles downloads.

Read the project's own contribution guidelines before starting. Please do not take Fjernsyn-specific problems to them; they do not maintain this fork.

You are welcome to use, study, change and share Fjernsyn under its [licence](LICENSE).
